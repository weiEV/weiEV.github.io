About Me

Hi, I am Wei Yi, a final-year Ph.D. candidate at Xidian University, advised by Prof. Jin Cao. In 2027, I will rejoin Dr. Yi Chen's group at the University of Hong Kong (HKU) as a postdoctoral fellow, where I previously spent a year as a visiting student.

My research focuses on cellular network security. Specifically, I explore new attack surfaces to reveal how attackers can abuse legitimate protocol behaviors defined in cellular standards. I also design practical defenses and work to incorporate them into 3GPP security standards.

I enjoy exchanging research ideas and exploring potential collaborations. Feel free to drop me an email if you'd like to chat!

Research Interests
